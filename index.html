Welcome

I am a Nanyang Assistant Professor (tenure-track) at the Nanyang Technological University (NTU) Singapore.

Previously, I was a postdoctoral research scientist at Columbia (Fall 2022 - Summer 2025), Ritt Assistant Professor at Columbia (Spring 2022), and a Gamelin Endowed Postdoctoral Fellow at the MSRI programme on Universality and Integrability in Random Matrix Theory and Interacting Particle Systems in Fall 2021. I obtained my PhD in 2021 from UC Berkeley, where I was advised by Alan Hammond and Shirshendu Ganguly.

My research interests lie in probability theory, particularly in the area of KPZ and stochastic growth models.
