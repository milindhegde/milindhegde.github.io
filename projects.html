Projects

As an undergraduate I undertook a project each summer under the guidance of a professor, as well as one for my final year. Here are the project reports I wrote for each, with more detailed descriptions below.

Final Year Project
2015 2014 2013

  • Final Year Project: The Fock Space and the Hilbert transform, under Prof. S. Thangavelu, IISc, Bangalore.Current Progress

    I am studying the Fock space (the space of entire functions which are square integrable with respect to the Gaussian measure) and an operator on it which is unitarily eqivalent to the Hilbert transform on \(L^2(\mathbb R)\). I am currently trying to show that it is bounded without using the Hilbert transform equvivalence. This is motivated by the following paper by Kehe Zhu: link.

  • 2015: Algebraic Number Theory and the Sign of the Gauss Sum, under Prof. Sandeep Varma, TIFR Mumbai.Project Report

    I studied algebraic number theory from the online notes by J. S. Milne, and completed the first six chapters, which includes important topics like the factorization of ideals in Dedekind domains, the finiteness of the class number, and Dirichlet's unit theorem.

    I also gave a 20 minute talk on the sign of the Gauss Sum, based on a blog post by Dr. David Speyer.

  • 2014: Graph Theory, Extremal Combinatorics, and The Complete Intersecton Theorem, under Prof. Niranjan Balachandran, IIT Bombay.Project Report

    This project consisted of four parts. In the first I studied graph theory from Introduction to Graph Theory by D. West, and covered topics up until colourings of graphs.

    In the second, I studied aspects of extremal set theory from Combinatorics of Finite Sets by I. Anderson, and covered the majority of the book. One theorem studied was the Erdős-Ko-Rado Theorem.

    Next I studied a generalization of the Erdős-Ko-Rado Theorem, The Complete Intersection Theorem, from Lectures on Advances in Combinatorics by R. Ahlswede and V. Blinovsky.

    Finally I spent time on a conjecture in extremal graph theory provided by my guide. While a little headway was made, there was insufficient time to obtain meaningful progress. A document with the progress is in the “Other Math” section.

  • 2013: Fluid Mechanics and the Plateau-Rayleigh Instability, under Prof. Arnab Rai Choudhuri, IISc. Project Report

    I first studied the basics of fluid mechanics from the text The Physics of Fluids and Plasmas: An Introduction for Astrophysicists by Prof. Arnab Rai Choudhuri, and then proceeded to study the Plateau-Rayleigh Instability, which is about the breakup of a vertical stream of fluid into smaller droplets.
